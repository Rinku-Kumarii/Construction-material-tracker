# Reflection — write this yourself, without AI

The assignment requires this half-page reflection to be written without AI. Use your own words and actual experience. Consider:
- What I misunderstood at first.
- What I would cut or simplify if starting again.
- Where AI helped with code/debugging.
- Where AI wasted time or made a wrong assumption.
- What I learned from the person and real records.
- What the tool cannot capture (stock consumption, quality, transport, wastage).

Replace these prompts with your own honest half-page reflection.
