# Research: Construction Material Requirement & Purchase Tracker

## Problem (based on the interview)
The interviewee works as a contractor on industrial building construction. Material quantities are estimated from drawings, and purchases are placed in stages as work progresses. Purchase records are kept manually in a site register. The interviewee wants to see the estimated requirement, quantity purchased so far, and remaining quantity to arrange. Supplier rates and quality are compared, and cement and steel prices can change.

2. **Spreadsheet (Excel/Google Sheets):** Can store estimates and purchases and calculate balances, but needs regular data entry and formula maintenance.
